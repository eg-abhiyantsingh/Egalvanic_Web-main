# [Web] An accepted resolution could not price its own quote line, and there was no company-wide place to work the issue funnel

**QA verdict — the headline behaviour works: an accepted resolution really is the pricing truth for its corrective quote line, the line badges `resolution` and names the resolution it came from, and Pull-Through Work gives the company-wide funnel with working stat cards, filters and bulk actions. Manual resolutions are born accepted and the Execute step is gone. Two defects: the price does **not** refresh when you reopen the quote after editing the resolution — only an explicit Edit Quote → Save & Regenerate repriced it (the ticket's "digest busts the reprice cache" claim did not hold on the reopen path); and a material whose quantity is indeterminate prices on the quote as **×0 / $0** rather than showing TBD. One finding: the Add-to-Quote gate is bypassable through Edit Quote, which put 28 unpriced corrective lines on a customer quote in one click. Five checklist steps could not be exercised on QA because the SCCR fix-flow the migrations create does not exist here.**

**Tested:** 2026-09-08 · `acme.qa.egalvanic.ai` build **V1.36**, bundle **`index-CSsDpG3c.js`** · tenant acme · Super Admin seat · live UI clicks with every request captured, plus the bundle and the service export-spec read directly.
**Ticket said "dev only (cicd/dev), not yet promoted to cicd/qa" — wrong for the web half.** Pull-Through Work, the issue workbench with accept/unaccept/manual resolutions and editable labor and materials, `plans/from-issues`, the `resolution` / `needs pick` badge code and the fix-flow rule editor (menu selection, AI guidance, evaluated materials) are all live on this build.

## Defects

### DEFECT 1 (High) — the quote keeps the stale price after the accepted resolution is edited
**Steps.** Accept a resolution and Add to Quote (quote reads $235). Back on the issue workbench, change the labor from 60 to 120 minutes (`PUT /api/issue-resolution/{id}/labor` → 200) and add a material. Reopen the quote.
**Actual.** The quote still reads **$235 sell / $145 cost**, the work-order line still says 60 min, and Manage Materials still lists only the original fuse. `GET /api/plans/{plan_id}` returns the old `content.pricing.totals`, every `est_mins` still 60, no trace of the new material, and `modified_at` unchanged at creation time. This survived a 25-second wait, a hard reload, a fresh navigation from the Quotes list (which also shows $235.00) and a second visit. Only **Edit Quote → Save & Regenerate** (`POST /api/plans/{id}/generate`) recomputed it, to **$335 sell / $155 cost** with `est_mins` 120 and the new material present.
**Expected.** Per the ticket, "the plan digest busts the reprice cache whenever a resolution is accepted or edited", so simply reopening the quote should show the refreshed amount.
**Why it matters.** The whole point of tier-0 pricing is that the fix the engineer chose and the amount the customer is quoted cannot disagree. Between the edit and a manual regenerate they disagree silently, and nothing on the quote hints that it is out of date. A quote sent in that window carries the wrong number.

### DEFECT 2 (Medium) — an indeterminate material quantity prices as zero on the quote
**Steps.** On an accepted resolution add a material with the quantity box cleared (stored as NULL, correctly). Regenerate the quote and open the Pricing tab.
**Actual.** The line reads "**×0 · $0 · $0**" and the material does not appear in Manage Materials at all. In the editable workbench its quantity is an empty input rather than the word TBD.
**Expected.** The ticket is explicit that these render as **TBD rather than as zero**, precisely so they are not mistaken for "none needed". The read-only rendering path does implement TBD; the quote's pricing table does not.
**Why it matters.** A part that is genuinely "count to be determined" reads on a customer-facing quote as a part costing nothing, which is exactly the misreading the caveat was written to prevent.

## Findings

### FINDING 3 (Medium) — the Add-to-Quote gate does not cover Edit Quote
**Add to Quote** correctly refuses to build a line without an accepted resolution (button disabled in the dialog, menu item disabled in bulk). But the quote's own **Edit Quote → issue picker** has no such gate: its master checkbox selected all 29 open issues in one click, and Save & Regenerate produced 29 lines, **28 of them "no work procedure for this service" with no price**, on a customer-facing Draft quote. The total stayed $335, so nothing was mispriced — but the unpriced corrective lines are exactly what step 11's negative case is meant to prevent, reached through a different door. The quote was restored to its single intended issue afterwards.

### FINDING 4 (Low) — one `POST …/generate` returned 401 and the retry succeeded
A regenerate answered **401** and the immediate retry answered 200 with no data lost — a session refresh landing mid-call. Recorded because it will look like a flake in any automated run of this flow.

### FINDING 5 (Low) — `/emps` still renders after the nav entry was retired
The Sales nav no longer offers EMPs, but typing `/emps` still loads the old "EMPs · Create EMP" list. If the retirement is meant to be complete, the route should redirect.
