_Video, 85 s, QA build index-oFAZQ3rK.js, 16 Nov 2026 ~18:58 IST. Facility Manager: avatar › Account & settings → lands on Site Health; typing /account does the same. Client Portal: same. Control: Super Admin gets the Account & settings page._

h3. Simple explanation
Portal users (Facility Manager and Client Portal) cannot open *Account & settings*. In the Maintenance Portal they click their avatar (bottom left) › *Account & settings*, and the page jumps straight back to *Site Health*. *Profile*, *Security* and *Notifications* never open, so a portal user cannot see their profile, set up two-factor sign-in, change their password settings or choose their email notifications. Internal users (Super Admin) get the page normally.

The two-factor prompt at sign-in even tells portal users: "You can set this up any time from Security in your account menu". That page is the one they can't reach.

*Where:* Maintenance Portal › avatar (bottom left) › Account & settings. Also any direct link to /account. The Profile / Security / Notifications tabs are /account?tab=… on that same path.

*Environment:* QA https://acme.qa.egalvanic.ai, company EG-ACME, build index-oFAZQ3rK.js, Chrome on macOS, 16 Nov 2026. First seen in a QA screen recording with Nency's portal account (site Android Site 2); reproduced with our own Facility Manager and Client Portal seats.

*Steps*
# Sign in as a Facility Manager portal user (QA seat abhiyant.singh+fm@egalvanic.com). You land in the Maintenance Portal.
# Open any portal page, for example Issues: https://acme.qa.egalvanic.ai/maintenance-portal/issues
# Click the avatar at the bottom left. The menu shows your name, the role "Facility Manager" and *Account & settings*.
# Click *Account & settings*.

*Actual*
* The page goes /account → /maintenance-portal → /maintenance-portal/overview (Site Health) in about 20 ms. The Account & settings page never shows. In Nency's recording the left menu "Account & settings / Profile / Security / Notifications" flashes for a moment with an empty page, then Site Health loads.
* Opening https://acme.qa.egalvanic.ai/account directly does the same. So do /account/profile, /account/security, /account/notifications and /settings.
* Same with the Client Portal seat (abhiyant.singh+clientportal@egalvanic.com).

*Expected:* the Account & settings page opens for portal users too, with Profile, Security and Notifications (Company can stay hidden for them).

*Controls*
* Super Admin, same build: avatar › Account & settings opens /account with Profile, Security, Notifications and Company.

*Possible cause (for the developer to confirm):*
* The avatar menu item calls navigate("/account").
* The guard that keeps portal users inside the portal only lets them stay on paths under "/maintenance-portal" and "/release-updates" (in the QA bundle: {{const ZFe=\[Ec,"/release-updates"\]}} with Ec="/maintenance-portal"; anything else → <Navigate to="/maintenance-portal" replace/>).
* "/account" is not on that list, so portal users are always sent back to the portal home, which then opens Site Health.
* Fix idea: add "/account" to the portal-allowed paths (and check that the Profile / Security / Notifications tabs work for portal roles), or give the portal its own account page and point the menu item there for portal users.

*Related:* ZP-5040 (Account & settings opens Profile), ZP-5292 (Account › Notifications empty "General" box).
