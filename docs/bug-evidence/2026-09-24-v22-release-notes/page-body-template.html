What customers get in V2.2

Twenty new things, grouped by where they live in the product, each with the clicks to find it and a picture of what it looks like. Everything in the main sections was checked working on QA on 23–24 September. The end of the page lists what was fixed, and what is not ready yet — so nobody promises it.

Links from emails

A work-order link opens the right work order, on the right site

A link to a work order — the kind a notification email carries, or one a colleague pastes — now opens that work order directly. If it belongs to a different site from the one on screen, the app switches to that site by itself, so the work order is never shown against the wrong site.

Where to find it
  1. Click a work-order link in a notification email
  2. The work order opens straight away
  3. If it belongs to another site, the site at the top changes to match

ZP-4030

Site walks

Journal walks — talk and shoot, then review on the web

A new kind of site walk alongside Count. With Journal, the technician simply talks and takes photos while walking the site; the notes and photos are kept in the walk, grouped by location, and read back on the web in Journal Review. Long notes now save in full.

Where to find it
  1. Click Sales in the left rail, then Site Walks
  2. Click New Site Walk and choose Journal instead of Count
  3. Journal walks carry a JOURNAL tag in the Site Walks list
  4. Open one to read its entries, location by location, in Journal Review

Worth knowing: journal walks are recorded on the mobile app; the web is where they are reviewed. Reading assets and condition out of a journal automatically is still being finished — on QA the review panel still says “Nothing interpreted yet”.

ZP-4068 · ZP-4208

Maintenance

Clearer answers on the maintenance pages: what is open, what is due, and what the standard asks for years ahead.

Open issues by type, on the Site Overview

The Site Overview has a new Open Issues by Type card beside Assets by Type: the site's open issues in one number, with the issue types listed next to it. The left-hand menu now gathers Maintenance Program, Compliance and Reports under their own MAINTENANCE heading.

Where to find it
  1. Click Site Data, then Site Overview
  2. The card sits to the right of Assets by Type
  3. Maintenance Program, Compliance and Reports are under MAINTENANCE in the menu

ZP-4038

Find an asset in the maintenance programme

The programme timeline has a search box. Type part of an asset's name and the timeline narrows to the matches — searched across the whole site, not just the rows already on screen. The footer says how many are shown.

Where to find it
  1. Click Site Data, then Maintenance Program
  2. Type in Search assets…
  3. The footer reads, for example, “Showing 2 of 3 assets”

ZP-4044

A plain answer when a deferral date is in the past

Deferring a scheduled service to a date that has already passed used to show a raw system error. It now says, in plain words, “The new due date must be in the future — pick tomorrow or later.” The form stays open so the date can be corrected, and nothing changes until a valid date is chosen.

Where to find it
  1. Click Site Data, then Maintenance Program
  2. Click Defer
  3. Pick an asset, a service and a new due date, then click Defer
  4. A past date is refused with the message above

ZP-4212

See what the standard asks for, years ahead — the Compliance Visualizer

A new Visualizer tab on Compliance lays out what the chosen maintenance standard prescribes for every asset, year by year and service by service — a dot for each service due. Group by, Filters, Export and full screen sit on its toolbar.

Where to find it
  1. Click Site Data, then Compliance
  2. Choose the standard at the top right — for example NFPA 70B 2026
  3. Click the Visualizer tab
  4. Use Group by and Filters to narrow it, and Export to take it away

Worth knowing: nothing shows until a standard is chosen — until then the tab reads “The standard prescribes nothing for this site's assets yet.”

ZP-4112

Condition assessment

Condition Assessment, reorganised

The Condition Assessment page opens on an Overview: assets assessed, open findings, assets at risk and the replacement cost of what is at risk, then Equipment Health split into Healthy, Assessment expired, At risk and Not assessed — assets whose assessment has lapsed now get their own bucket instead of counting as healthy. Conditions use one scale throughout (Condition 1, 2, 3 and Non-Serviceable), and the page says how many assets had their condition revised automatically in the last 30 days.

Where to find it
  1. Click Site Data, then Condition Assessment
  2. Overview is the first tab
  3. Under Condition Distribution, click See what changed to list the automatic revisions

ZP-4043 · ZP-4041 · ZP-4084

Work through findings and assets in one place

Findings lists the at-risk assets first, then every open finding with its type, condition and recommended action — and a pencil to edit it where it stands. Asset Details lists every asset with filters for location, class, shutdown rule, condition and assessment, and Bulk Ops for acting on several at once.

Where to find it
  1. Click Site Data, then Condition Assessment
  2. Findings tab: click the pencil on a finding to edit it
  3. Asset Details tab: filter the list, then use Bulk Ops to select several

ZP-4039

The issue report and the assessment report, separately

The issue report is now a report of its own, with its own card in Reports: every issue at the site on its own page — priority, findings, thermal analysis and photos where they exist — behind a one-table summary. The Condition Assessment page has two buttons, Export issue report and Export assessment report.

Where to find it
  1. Click Site Data, then Reports — the Issue Report card
  2. Or on Condition Assessment, use the two export buttons at the top right

ZP-4113

Work orders

General comes first when creating a work order

In the Create Work Order wizard, the First service to perform list now starts with General, the most common choice, instead of burying it at the bottom.

Where to find it
  1. Click Operations, then Work Orders, then Create Work Order
  2. Fill in the first step and click Continue
  3. Open First service to perform — General is at the top

ZP-4347

The IR tick box shows on child assets before any photo exists

On an infrared work order, a child asset that is in scope for infrared — a panelboard, or a breaker at condition 2 or 3 with three or more poles — now shows its IR tick box straight away, at 0 photos, instead of only after the first IR photo is added. A child that is out of scope (for example a breaker with no pole count or condition recorded) shows none, the same as it would at the top level.

Where to find it
  1. Open an Infrared Thermography work order
  2. Expand a panelboard to see the assets under it
  3. In-scope children show the IR Photos tick box at 0

ZP-4309

Mark As works on the location view

Marking several assets done for a service at once — Bulk Ops → Mark As.. — now works when the assets list is grouped by location. The menu offers only the services the selected assets actually carry, and the ticks are still there after a reload.

Where to find it
  1. Open a work order and stay on Assets
  2. Click Bulk Ops and tick the assets
  3. Click Mark As.. and choose a service, done or not done

ZP-4062

Services & devices

Edit many service methods at once

A service's methods can be seen in one flat table with All methods — each asset class's method, labour, estimate, forms and equipment side by side — and several can be ticked and edited together with Edit N selected.

Where to find it
  1. Click Builder, then Services, and open a service
  2. Switch from By class to All methods
  3. Tick the methods to change
  4. Click Edit N selected

ZP-4109

Rename a service in one step

A service can be renamed straight from its menu, without going through Update service.

Where to find it
  1. Click Builder, then Services, and open the service
  2. Click ⋯ at the top right
  3. Choose Rename

ZP-4152

Rules for one particular device

Service rules can now be set for a specific device from the equipment library — one breaker model, say — not only for a whole asset class, and they have their own page under Admin → Devices. Adding one goes service first, then asset class, then device, so the search only looks in the right part of the library.

Where to find it
  1. Click Admin, then Devices
  2. Click Add device
  3. Choose the service, then the asset class, then find the device
  4. Click Continue to rules

Worth knowing: the Devices list is empty until the first device rule is added.

ZP-4110 · ZP-4111

Assets & engineering

Junction boxes stop asking for panel details

Creating or editing a Junction Box no longer asks for panel-only details such as mains type, panel type or phase configuration. Its Engineering section asks for System Voltage and the attributes that apply to a junction box. A Panelboard still asks for all of them.

Where to find it
  1. Click Site Data, then Assets, then Create Asset
  2. Choose the class Junction Box
  3. Scroll to Engineering: System Voltage and its own attributes only

ZP-4167

A real manufacturer list for breakers

The Manufacturer field on a circuit breaker now offers names to pick from — 52 of them on QA, from ABB and Allen-Bradley onwards — instead of starting empty.

Where to find it
  1. Create or edit a Circuit Breaker
  2. In Engineering, open Manufacturer

ZP-4170

Core attributes can apply to some subtypes only

When setting up an asset class, each core attribute can be limited to certain subtypes — the column reads Subtypes (empty = all) — so, for example, a field that only makes sense for one kind of switch is not asked of every switch.

Where to find it
  1. Click Admin, then Asset Classes, and open a class
  2. Under Core Attributes, click Add
  3. Pick subtypes in Subtypes, or leave it empty for all

ZP-4171

Settings Verifier, from the export menu

The single-line diagram's Export menu has a new Settings Verifier entry. It opens the steps for auditing protective-device settings and provides the EG Utils installer that runs them.

Where to find it
  1. Click Engineering, then SLD
  2. Click Export at the bottom of the diagram
  3. Choose Settings Verifier

ZP-4131

Customers

Customer search that also finds sites

The Customers list now loads a page at a time and searches on the server, so it stays quick on tenants with hundreds of customers — and the search matches site names too, showing each site under the customer that owns it.

Where to find it
  1. Click Admin, then Customers
  2. Type a customer or a site name in the search box
  3. Matching sites appear under their customer
