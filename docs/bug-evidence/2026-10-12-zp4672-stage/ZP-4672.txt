# *Codes are kept:* after a page reload, the "No QR code yet" list on test site went from 1,907 to 1,846, exactly the 61 codes saved. Searching the 50-batch finds all 50.
# *Double-click Save codes:* 1 request, no duplicate codes.
# *Change an existing code:* "Change existing QR codes?" appears, shows old and new code, and closes after *Change codes*.

*Note for the developer (not a failure):* save time grows about 30 ms per code. The app sends up to 1,000 codes per request, so a 1,000-code save would take about 30 s. Not tested; worth one check together with ZP-4671.
