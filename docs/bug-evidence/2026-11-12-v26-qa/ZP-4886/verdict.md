## Enhancement suggestions
- Done text says "1 views and 0 SLD links created." — pluralise ("1 view") (seen on the 158-node run). Cosmetic.
- The rerun is sequential (each delete/create ~0.6–0.8 s); parallelising the view/link writes would cut a large site's wait.
- A 150-sibling tree lays out as one very long row (fit view makes every box unreadable); consider wrapping wide sibling sets.

## Mobile impact
Generated views and SLD links are data: an iOS/Android tester should open one generated view on mobile and tap an SLD-link stub
(jump to the other view), and check that `hidden_by:"layout"` edges show as stubs, not lines.
