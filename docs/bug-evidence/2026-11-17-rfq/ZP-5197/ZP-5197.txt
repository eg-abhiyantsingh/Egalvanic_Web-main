*Steps*
# Assets tab > + (Add Assets) > New Asset.
# Quick: tap Photos, Infrared, then Basic.
# Pick asset class Circuit Breaker > Detailed. Tap Engineering, Commercial, Connections, Infrared and Notes.
# Scroll the form by hand and watch the highlighted link.
# Compare with an existing asset page: "29 july abhiyant asset".

*Actual*
* New Asset has an "Asset sections" row. Quick: Basic, Photos, Engineering, Infrared. Detailed: Basic, Photos, Engineering, Commercial, Connections, Infrared, Notes (the > arrow slides the row).
* Each link jumps to its section and is highlighted. Infrared and Notes are the last sections, so they land at the end of the form.
* While scrolling, the highlight follows the section at the top (Basic > Photos > Engineering > Commercial), and the row slides to keep it in view.
* The asset page has the same chips plus Issues and Maintenance, which need a saved record.

*Small notes (not blocking)*
* Quick mode has no Engineering header, but the Engineering link is still shown. It lands on the "Trust the Photos" card.
* Connections and Infrared are not highlighted while scrolling by hand: at the bottom of the form the highlight jumps to Notes. Tapping them works.
* The > arrow covers the right edge of the last visible link, so a tap on that edge slides the row instead.

*Not covered:* tablet width (side list) and landscape. Classes other than Circuit Breaker.
