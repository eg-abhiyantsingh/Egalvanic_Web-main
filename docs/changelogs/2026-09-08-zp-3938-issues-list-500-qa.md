# 2026-09-08 — QA: ZP-3938 [Web] Every company Issues list returned 500 once background resolution processing shipped

**Prompt:** the ticket text with its 5-step QA review ("test this ticket too").

## What was done
1. **Loaded both pages the regression killed** — the company Issues register and Pull-Through Work each render their grids on every visit, repeatedly, across a long session. The list call behind them was incidentally hammered as the surface underneath a dozen other checks.
2. **Called the endpoint directly** — 200 with 1,059 issues.
3. **Looked for the field the ticket names and did not find it** — rows carry a processing flag *without* the timestamp suffix the review step asks a tester to confirm.
4. **Produced the empty case honestly** — this tenant has over a thousand issues, so an unmatchable search was used: empty list, stat counts intact, no error. A genuinely issue-free company was not available, and the report says so.
5. Verdict, evidence, artifact page.

## Results (short)
- **PASS** — the 500 is gone on this build; neither page fails.
- **FINDING 1 (Low, documentation)** — the serialized field is not the one the ticket names, so step 3 cannot be confirmed as written. Either it was renamed after the ticket or the text is stale; worth reconciling so the next tester is not hunting a key that always reads undefined.
- Not run: an issue caught mid-evaluation (nothing was in flight, and the field as named does not exist to check).

## Depth notes (learning)
- **A one-line serializer fix deserves repetition, not depth.** The strongest evidence here is that the call succeeded dozens of times across hours of unrelated work — an intermittent 500 would have shown itself.
- **Check the field name, not just the symptom.** The page renders either way; noticing that the key differs from the ticket is what stops the next tester wasting an hour.
- **Approximate the empty state and label the approximation.** An unmatchable filter is a fair stand-in for zero rows; pretending it was a zero-issue company would not be.
